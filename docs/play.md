# Play

Click the game, then use the keys:

- Red tank, left wheel: `q` forward, `z` backward, `a` shoot
- Red tank, right wheel: `u` forward, `m` backward, `j` shoot
- Blue tank, left wheel: `r` forward, `v` backward, `f` shoot
- Blue tank, right wheel: `p` forward, `/` backward, `;` shoot

A tank drives straight when both its wheels move the same way,
and turns when only one wheel moves.
A tank fires when both its players press shoot
and its previous bullet has left the arena.
